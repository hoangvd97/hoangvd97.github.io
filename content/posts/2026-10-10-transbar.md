---
title: "Transbar: Quickly translate in Chrome's sidebar"
tags: [extensions]
---
<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">Transbar is a Chrome extension I made that puts a translator in the browser's side panel.</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">The panel stays open next to the page, so you can read, translate and keep reading without switching tabs.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">Why did you make it? There are already some AI extensions that can translate, and do a lot more than that.</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">I know that, but when I'm studying or reading docs (usually in English), I just want to look up the meaning (or pronunciation) of a word I don't know (or have forgotten), and don't need anything else.</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">So I made Transbar as minimal and as handy as possible.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">Okay, so what does it do?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">Click the Transbar icon in the toolbar, or press **Alt+T** (**⌥T** on a Mac), and a translator opens in the sidebar. It translates as you type, in 100+ languages, and detects the source language by itself.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">What if the word is on the page I'm reading?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">Select it, right-click and choose **Translate with Transbar**. The panel opens with that text already filled in.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">And the pronunciation?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">Press **Listen** to hear the original text or the translation, in 65 languages. You can also copy the translation and swap the two languages in one click.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">Do I have to pick my languages every time?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">No, it remembers your language pair. The text you were typing is still there when you reopen the panel.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">What happens to the text I type?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">It goes only to Google Translate, to translate. Transbar has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own, and your settings stay in your browser.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">Nice! Where can I get it?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">Install it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/transbar/mmammedlnghmfikdcoilohhjhcefobld). It needs Chrome 116 or newer.</span>

<span class="bubble-right" data-name="You">And if I want to see how it works?</span>

<span class="bubble-left" data-name="Hoang">I've published the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/hoangvd97/Transbar) under the MIT License. You can find the technical details and how to develop it there. Thanks!</span>
